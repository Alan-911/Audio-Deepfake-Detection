Research Insights
Why Mask Separation Works
Synthetic artifacts reside in the speech signal, not background noise. Prior models trained on dataset-specific environments fail on unseen generators because they overfit to acoustic conditions rather than speech-level forgery cues.
Frobenius Orthogonality Loss
Enforces ‖M_s · M_eᵀ‖_F → 0, ensuring the two masks capture non-overlapping information. This is the core regularizer that enables out-of-distribution generalization on unseen spoof generators.
CompSpoofV2 Benchmark
Evaluated on 250K clips across 5 classes (Genuine + 4 spoof types A–D). Test set contains generators not seen during training, making macro-F1 the key generalization metric.
